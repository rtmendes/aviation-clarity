# Aviation Clarity™ Master PRD

## Mission
Make aviation easier to understand, teach, grow, and live.

## Users
Student pilots, CFIs, flight schools, aviation businesses, pilots, and aviation creators.

## Product pillars
1. Learn — simplify complex aviation knowledge.
2. Teach — help CFIs communicate concepts through multiple modalities.
3. Grow — help aviation businesses become visible and convert demand.
4. Live — help pilots manage career, family, scheduling, transitions, and wellbeing.
5. AI — provide aviation-specific workflows and operating systems.
6. Joy — make the professional aviation journey more engaging without compromising safety.

## Core loop
Problem → research → verify → translate → teach → tool → measure → improve → productize.

## MVP
Command Center, Research Lab, Content Factory, Agent Studio, Knowledge Base, Product Lab, QA Center, Content Calendar, Aviation Explanation Engine.

## Success metrics
Completed learning sessions, diagnostic completions, qualified leads, paid conversion, production cycle time, verified-content rate, retention, and product revenue.

## Safety requirement
Regulatory, operational, medical, aircraft-specific, and safety-critical claims require authoritative source verification and appropriate qualified human review before publication or operational use.
