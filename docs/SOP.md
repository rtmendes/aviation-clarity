# Master SOP

1. Capture a specific audience problem.
2. Classify the problem and safety sensitivity.
3. Research using authoritative sources.
4. Verify time-sensitive and regulatory claims.
5. Translate complexity into a simple mental model.
6. Produce the core asset.
7. Generate derivatives for YouTube, podcast, short-form, article, email, and social.
8. Run QA for factual accuracy, sourcing, safety, claims, accessibility, and brand voice.
9. Route safety-critical material to qualified human review.
10. Publish approved assets.
11. Measure engagement, learning completion, conversion, and revenue.
12. Feed performance back into topic prioritization and product development.
