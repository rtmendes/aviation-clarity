# Aviation Clarity™ Book-Series Production Architecture

## Series 1 — Train the Trainer

Four ~100-page books designed around multimodal learning and exam preparation.

### Book 1 — Decode Aviation
How to turn technical aviation concepts into plain-language mental models.

### Book 2 — Remember Aviation
Memory architecture: retrieval practice, spaced repetition, mnemonics, visual models, stories and scenario-based learning.

### Book 3 — Teach Aviation
CFI-focused lesson design: explain, demonstrate, question, diagnose misunderstanding and adapt modality.

### Book 4 — Pass the Test & Checkride
Exam/checkride preparation using concept maps, error analysis, practice questions and confidence-building routines.

## Series 2 — Aviation Marketing

Four ~100-page books covering positioning, visibility, demand generation and conversion for aviation businesses.

## Series 3 — Pilot Life OS

Four ~100-page books covering career planning, schedule resilience, family/life logistics, wellbeing and change management.

## Series 4 — The Fun, Safe Pilot

Four ~100-page books covering aviation culture, enjoyable skill-building, adventure planning, community and professional safety-minded fun.

## Production rule
Every chapter becomes a content cluster: long-form video, podcast segment, short-form clips, article, email, worksheet/checklist and product opportunity. Technical aviation claims must pass source verification before publication.
