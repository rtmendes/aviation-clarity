Where the estimate is soft

  • Unpublished rates. Six schools publish no hourly rate. They are modeled at your two “assumed” rates and tagged modeled — they never win the “cheapest published” tile. Phone them, then type the real rate into the row and the tag turns into your quote.
  • Examiner fees and waits. Only two fees here are real: Richmor holds FAA Part 141 examining authority so its checkride carries no outside DPE fee, and Luke Weathers confirmed $600. Every other school uses your “examiner fee” input. The wait weeks are estimates derived from each school’s checkride signals — on-site DPE, Part 141 status — not quotes. Override both per school.
  • Distance is straight-line from the 10030 centroid (40.8184, −73.9423) to the airport, so it understates real driving in the NYC metro. That is what the commute multiplier is for — or type your actual drive miles into the row.
  • Package schools (ATP $410/hr, LuvsFlying $400/hr) bundle instructor time into the hourly rate, so their instructor cost is set to $0 and they are tagged package. Not directly comparable to à-la-carte pricing.
  • Dry rates. Three Wing is modeled at its $210/hr all-in figure ($130 dry + ~$80 fuel). Richmor’s $205 excludes a fuel surcharge, so its total reads low — tagged excl. fuel.

Simulator credit

Only time in an FAA-approved BATD/AATD flown with an instructor counts toward the certificate, capped at 2.5 hours under 14 CFR 61.109(k) for Part 61. Home flight simulators do not count. The slider only applies to schools with a qualified device and a known hourly rate: Luke Weathers $35, Airborne Elite $40, Long Island Aviators $70, Air Fleet $80, Global $85, Richmor $165.

Deadline fit

Projected days = (training weeks × (1 + your cancellation buffer) + examiner wait weeks) × 7, compared against your deadline input. Weather, maintenance and aircraft scheduling in the New York area routinely eat 20–25% of planned lessons, which is what the buffer models.
