# FAA written-test sequencing ahead of the 27 October 2026 IRA format change

**Review gate:** regulatory claims are cited to current eCFR and to the FAA's own
Airman Testing Community Advisory. Re-verify before publication.

## Three corrections to a widely-repeated premise

| Common claim | What the primary source says |
| --- | --- |
| The change lands 20 October 2026 | **27 October 2026** |
| The FAA moves to "AI-driven exams" | It is the **Graphics Conversion Project** — images embedded in questions instead of a printed supplement. The [June 2026 advisory](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/June_2026_Edition.pdf) does not mention AI. "Automatic Item Generation" is third-party framing. |
| All written tests change | On that date, only **IRA** and **UAG** gain image-based questions not found in the test supplements. Test supplements are **not** being withdrawn: "The date for removing testing supplements from test centers has not yet been set." |

Verbatim, June 2026 ATCA pp. 5–6:

> Beginning October 27, 2026, the Instrument Rating Airplane (IRA) and Unmanned
> Aircraft General-Small (UAG) tests will include image-based questions not found
> in the test supplements.

Independent corroboration from a vendor with no incentive to say it —
[Sheppard Air's private pilot page](https://sheppardair.com/private.htm): "The
Private Pilot tests are not undergoing any of the turmoil/chaos that all other
FAA pilot testing is experiencing."

## The governing constraint: knowledge tests expire

[14 CFR 61.39(a)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-A/section-61.39):
the practical test must be completed within the **24 calendar months** following
the month the knowledge test was passed. A test passed in October 2026 expires
**31 October 2028**. A passed written is not recorded on a certificate and is not
visible to an airline; it only unlocks a checkride.

| Written | Expires | Practical it gates | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Private (PAR) | 31 Oct 2028 | PPL, target Feb 2027 | Ample margin |
| Instrument (IRA) | 31 Oct 2028 | IR inside a 13-month program from Mar 2027 | Comfortable |
| Commercial (CAX) | 31 Oct 2028 | Commercial — requires 250 hours | **Thin.** Taken first, used last. |

## Recommendation

**Two writtens in the sprint, not three.**

- **Private (PAR)** — take it. Endorsement is bundled with the ground school.
- **Instrument (IRA)** — take it before 27 October. The only test with a real
  deadline, and the historically lowest-pass-rate test, so rote bank prep earns
  the most here.
- **Commercial (CAX)** — defer to 2027. No October deadline; starts the 24-month
  clock against the rating completed last; requires a separate endorsement that
  is a harder ask pre-solo.

## Eligibility and the endorsement gate

No regulation requires holding a private pilot certificate before sitting the IRA
or CAX knowledge test.
[61.35](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-A/section-61.35)
requires an instructor endorsement (where the part requires one) and ID showing
the applicant meets the age requirement before the test report expires.

| Test | Rule | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private | 61.35(a)(1) | Ground training or home study + endorsement |
| Instrument | [61.65(a)(3)–(4)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-B/section-61.65) | Ground training or home-study course **plus** a logbook/training-record endorsement certifying preparedness |
| Commercial | [61.123(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-F/section-61.123) | Instructor who conducted the ground training or reviewed the home study must endorse; applicant must be 18 |

Sheppard Air does not issue endorsements. Securing a CFII willing to endorse a
pre-solo student for the IRA is the critical path item, and must be resolved in
week one or the deadline plan is void.

## Career-pathway conflict to resolve

[Destination 225° Cadet](https://careers.southwestair.com/us/en/D225Cadet)
requires external applicants to "hold no higher than a private pilot rating when
applying to or starting the program." Knowledge tests are not ratings and carry
no risk. A **completed instrument rating** before program start may cross that
line. Wording is ambiguous; requires written confirmation from Southwest.

## Counter-argument to record

Rote question-bank preparation is the correct trade under a hard deadline, but
the [ACS](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/training_testing/testing/acs/acs_faq.pdf)
expects the evaluator to revisit missed knowledge-test subject areas during the
oral. The Graphics Conversion Project specifically degrades recognition-based
recall of supplement figures. Chart-reading competence is the durable skill.
