# Jeppesen Best-in-Class EFB vs ForeFlight for a student pilot

**Review gate:** regulatory and operational claims below are sourced to primary
vendor and FAA pages and must be re-verified before publication. Prices are
volatile and were read from the vendors' own pages on **2026-09-06**.

## Headline finding

The comparison as usually framed is not a real choice.

1. **Jeppesen's "Best-in-Class EFB" has no individual SKU.** The
   [pricing page](https://ww2.jeppesen.com/navigation-solutions/jeppesens-best-in-class-efb/pricing-best-in-class-efb/)
   lists four tiers (Standard, Essential, Preferred, Prime) and publishes **no
   prices**; every tier routes to
   [Contact Sales](https://etracking.jeppesen.com/contact-sales-commercial).
   The underlying product is [FliteDeck Pro](https://ww2.jeppesen.com/navigation-solutions/flitedeck-pro/),
   an airline/fleet EFB licensed by contract and provisioned through an
   operator's Ground Controls portal.
2. **Jeppesen's GA app is discontinued.** Mobile FliteDeck (JeppFD) received no
   chart or data updates after AIRAC 2401, which expired **2024-02-22**, and was
   removed from the App Store; Mobile FliteDeck VFR was sunset **2020-03-15**.
   Jeppesen's own
   [transition page](https://www.foreflight.com/campaigns/jeppesen-mfd-transition/)
   directs displaced GA pilots to ForeFlight.
3. **They are the same company.** Thoma Bravo closed its **$10.55B** acquisition
   of Boeing's Digital Aviation Solutions business (Jeppesen, ForeFlight,
   AerData, OzRunways) on **2025-10-31**; the combined entity relaunched as
   [Jeppesen ForeFlight](https://foreflight.com/blog/jeppesen-foreflight-launches-as-a-standalone-company-to-redefine-the-future-of-aviation-software).
   ForeFlight's global navigation database is Jeppesen-sourced in every tier.

The decision that remains is **which ForeFlight tier**.

## Recommendation

**ForeFlight Essential, $260/yr.** Rationale specific to an ab-initio student at
Essex County Airport (KCDW), Fairfield NJ — [Century Air](https://centuryair.com/):

- NY Class B shelves overlie the practice area; Airspace **Profile View** is the
  direct mitigation and is Essential-only.
- Terrain rises west/north into the Highlands; **Hazard Advisor** and automatic
  terrain/obstacle alerts are Essential-only.
- Instrument training on the same fleet follows within ~12–18 months;
  geo-referenced plates, Plates on Maps and Dynamic Procedures are Essential-only.
  Buying Essential now removes a second purchase decision through CFI.
- Premium's differentiators (performance profiles, autorouting, TOLD, PDC/D-ATIS,
  JetFuelX) address turbine/dispatch problems a C172 does not have.

**Affordability path.** Starter → Essential is not a trap: ForeFlight applies a
**pro-rated credit** for unused time when upgrading on their website
([support](https://support.foreflight.com/hc/en-us/articles/214615678-When-upgrading-a-ForeFlight-Mobile-subscription-is-credit-applied-from-the-current-subscription)).
Three-year spend: Essential from day one **$780**; Starter then upgrade at first
cross-country **~$700** (estimate — exact credit computed by ForeFlight);
Premium from day one **$1,170**.

**Do not buy the Jeppesen $149 chart add-on as a student.** Airlines issue and
train their own EFB and chart format during indoc.

## Regulatory

[FAA AC 91-78A](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_91-78A.pdf)
permits EFB use in lieu of paper reference material in all phases of Part 91
flight, provided the EFB does not replace equipment required by Part 91. A
backup (second device or paper) is **recommended, not required**, under Part 91.
School policy may be stricter.
